How ClassroomAI Works

Get started in under 60 seconds. No complex setup required.

1

Teacher Starts a Session

The teacher logs in, sets a topic, uploads lecture materials, and starts a live session. Students get an email invite automatically.

2

Students Join & Interact

Students enter the session code and instantly have access to the AI tutor, flashcards, and quizzes — all based on the lecture content.

3

Learn, Compete & Grow

Students earn points, climb the leaderboard, and revise with flashcards. Teachers monitor engagement through live analytics.
